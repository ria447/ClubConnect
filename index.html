Technical Innovation and Skill Enhancement Cell (TISEC)

The cell aims to promote technical skills among students by organizing workshops, seminars, and hands-on training sessions in various engineering domains. It strives to create an environment where students are encouraged to think creatively, innovate, and develop practical solutions to real-world problems through projects, hackathons, and competitions. TISEC focuses on enhancing the professional development of students by organizing events like guest lectures, industrial visits, and career guidance sessions to help them understand industry trends and requirements.

AARVAK: The Technical Society of VSE&T

AARVAK is the official technical society of VIPS-TC It organises unique hackathons, e-sports events and fun and interactive tech games like tech bingo that recently happened. The society encourages its members to take initiatives and work on their skills through within society competitions and group projects.


Technical Clubs

CyberYoddha: The Cyber Security Club

To build a proactive, skill-driven community that fosters excellence in cyber defense, digital forensics, and cybersecurity innovation, while shaping future leaders in domains such as ethical hacking, security analysis, incident response, and information assurance, and contributing meaningfully to the national and global cybersecurity ecosystem.

CODEX: The CSE Club

To build a generation of confident and capable coders by nurturing strong foundations in programming, algorithmic thinking, and real-world application through collaborative projects and open-source contributions.

ADVAIT: The Machine Learning Club

To help students learn and explore the world of AI through hands-on projects, workshops, and teamwork. We want to make learning AI fun, practical, and useful for solving real-life problems.

EONICS: Electronics and IoT Club

To encourage students to explore the world of hardware, IoT, and embedded systems. The club provides a platform for students to explore emerging technologies through workshops, technical events, competitions, and hands on projects.

ChipCrafters: VLSI Design Club (Electronics Engineering)

A student-driven technical group focused on practising VLSI Design, semiconductor technology and professional industry standard chip design tools. The club aims to provide students with practical exposure to industry relevant IC design tools and simulation platforms beyond the classroom curriculum. Through collaborative learning the club helps students to develop practical skills and prepare for careers in the semiconductor industry



Other Communities

IEEE Student Branch

The IEEE Student Branch aims to foster technical innovation, research culture, and professional development among students through workshops, competitions, and collaborative projects. It provides a platform for engineering and technology students to enhance their technical skills, network with professionals, and access industry-leading resources. It fosters innovation through workshops, competitions, and mentorship, offering a competitive edge through professional development, networking, and industry connections.

Quantum Community

To cultivate a strong foundation in quantum science and technology among undergraduate students by integrating interdisciplinary learning and research-oriented thinking. This student community aims to prepare future engineers for emerging quantum technologies such as quantum computing, quantum communication, and quantum sensing. Through workshops, expert lectures, and collaborative projects, we strive to bridge academic knowledge with industry and research advancements in the global quantum ecosystem.

ALFRESCO: Sports Society

Alfresco is the official sports committee of VSET, dedicated to promoting physical fitness, team spirit, and a healthy competitive environment among students. It organizes a wide range of annual sports events including badminton, kho-kho, kabaddi, futsal, 100-meter race, relay race, carrom, table tennis, etc. Through these activities, Alfresco encourages students to showcase their athletic talents, build camaraderie, and embrace the values of sportsmanship and perseverance.
